## Full description
<!-- at most 4000 characters. Plain paragraphs and CAPITALISED headings; Play renders no markdown.
     Say what the app does, what it stores, that it works offline, and that it is paid once. -->
Make passport, visa and ID photos on your phone with no subscription. Cropmark sizes the photo to the document's own rule, checks head size and eye line before you save, and writes the exact pixels and file size an online form asks for or a print sheet with cut lines.
Every rule was checked against an official source, and each document shows that source and the date it was checked.
Your face never leaves the phone: Cropmark has no internet permission.

One-time purchase. No ads, no subscription, no account. Works fully offline.

A camera frame that measures
The camera shows where your crown, chin and eyes must fall, and the button says Take photo when your face fits. Shoot with the volume buttons, a Bluetooth selfie remote or a 3 or 10 second timer, on either camera.

Checks before you save
One face, head height, eye line, centring, tilt, facing the camera, eyes open, mouth closed, exposure, uneven lighting, plain background and enough pixels to print.

8 documents
US passport and DS-160 visa photo, India passport applications made abroad, India e-Visa, OCI card and PAN card, Canada visitor visa and China visa. Add your own size in millimetres or pixels, with its own resolution.

Edits follow the issuer
The US passport and visa, Canada visa, India passport and OCI rules reject photos changed with software, so for those documents Cropmark only crops and sizes the photo you took against a plain light wall. For the India e-Visa, PAN card, China visa and your own sizes you can replace the background with white, light blue, light grey or a light colour, with hair edges refined on the phone.

Form files and print sheets
Exact pixels, the right dpi and a file size limit such as 240 KB, with no location or camera data in the file. 4x6 and 5x7 JPEG sheets for photo kiosks, A4 and Letter PDF sheets with a 50 mm check ruler. Save to your phone or share to any app.

History and backup
Every photo is kept with its edits, so you can save it in another size later without retaking it. Back up everything to one file and restore it on another phone. Counter mode returns to the camera after every save.

Cropmark is not affiliated with any government. The issuing office decides whether a photo is accepted.

Not included: glasses detection, submitting photos online, or ordering prints.
